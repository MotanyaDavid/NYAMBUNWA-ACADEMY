Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Nyambunwa Academy ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our students, parents, staff, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and child's name/age (when submitting admission or contact forms).
  • Employment Data: CVs, cover letters, and professional details (when applying for a job).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and anonymous usage statistics to help us improve our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process admission inquiries and respond to messages.
  • Evaluate job applications.
  • Send newsletters and important school updates (if you have subscribed).
  • Comply with legal obligations and school policies.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. All data is stored on secure servers, and access is restricted to authorized school administration personnel only.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may only share information if required to do so by law or by the Ministry of Education.

5. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to school record-keeping laws).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing/newsletters at any time.
