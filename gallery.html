School Anthem

The Nyambunwa Academy anthem is a cherished tradition sung with pride by students, staff, and alumni at every major school event.

"Excel for Scholarship"

Verse 1:
In the heart of Nairobi we stand,
A beacon of learning across the land.
With minds ablaze and hearts so true,
We strive for greatness in all we do.

Chorus:
Excel for scholarship!
With integrity and resilience.
Nyambunwa Academy, our guiding light,
Building leaders, burning bright.

Verse 2:
From Kindergarten to Senior years,
We conquer challenges, overcome fears.
With compassion deep and service true,
The future shines in me and you.

(Repeat Chorus)
