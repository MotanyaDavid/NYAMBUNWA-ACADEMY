Portal Support

Common questions and guidance for accessing your portal.

Forgotten Password?

Click the "Forgot Password" link on your portal's login page. A reset link will be sent to your registered email address. If you don't receive it within 5 minutes, check your spam folder.

New User?

New students and parents receive their login credentials via email upon enrollment. Staff receive credentials during orientation. Contact the school office if you haven't received yours.

Technical Support

For technical assistance, email nyambunwaacademy@gmail.com or call +254 757 862 075 during working hours. Our IT team responds within 4 hours on weekdays.

Still Need Assistance?

Our team is happy to walk you through the login process or troubleshoot any issues you may encounter.
