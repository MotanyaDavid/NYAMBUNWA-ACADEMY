Choosing the right school for your child is one of the most important decisions you will ever make. At Nyambunwa Academy, we are honoured that you are considering us. Our admissions process is designed to be warm, transparent, and thorough.

How to Apply

Our Admission Process

A simple, transparent 5-step journey to becoming part of the Nyambunwa family.

1

Submit Inquiry

Fill out the online inquiry form below or call our admissions office.

2

School Tour

Visit our campus, tour the facilities, and meet our leadership team.

3

Entrance Assessment

Your child sits for a grade-appropriate assessment in core subjects.

4

Family Interview

A friendly conversation with the Principal about your child's future.

5

Offer & Registration

Successful candidates receive an admission offer letter to complete registration.
