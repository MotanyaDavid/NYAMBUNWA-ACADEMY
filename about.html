Our Journey

Our History

From humble beginnings in 1995 with 45 students and 6 teachers in a rented facility, Nyambunwa Academy has grown into one of Kisii's most respected private educational institutions.

Our Mission

To provide a transformative education that develops academically excellent, morally grounded, and socially responsible leaders who will positively impact their communities and the world.

Our Vision

To be a centre of educational excellence in Africa, producing generations of ethical leaders, innovative thinkers, and compassionate citizens who shine in every sphere of life.

A Word From

Our Principal

Welcome to Nyambunwa Academy.

For over three decades, it has been my privilege to lead an institution where children do not just learn — they thrive. When we opened our doors in 1995 with a handful of students and a big dream, we committed ourselves to a simple but powerful belief: every child carries unique potential, and it is our sacred duty to nurture it.

At Nyambunwa Academy, we do not simply prepare students for examinations. We prepare them for life. Our classrooms are vibrant spaces of inquiry, our sports fields teach resilience and teamwork, our chapels nurture the spirit, and our corridors echo with laughter, respect, and a sense of belonging.

We believe that education is a partnership — between dedicated teachers, supportive parents, and motivated students. When these three work together, extraordinary things happen. Our consistent academic results, our alumni's achievements, and the trust parents place in us year after year testify to this.

Whether you are a prospective parent exploring the right school for your child, an educator seeking to join a passionate team, or a visitor curious about who we are — you are welcome here. I invite you to explore our website, visit our campus, and experience firsthand what makes Nyambunwa Academy special.

Together, we shine.

Dr. Jane Muthoni
